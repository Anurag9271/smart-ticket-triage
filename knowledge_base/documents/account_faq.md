# NovaDesk Customer Support – Account FAQ

> **Demo knowledge base:** NovaDesk is a fictional company used for this project. The policies below are sample policies created for testing the Smart Customer Support Ticket Triage & Auto-Responder.

## 1. How can I reset my password?

Customers can select Forgot Password on the login page and enter their registered email address. A password reset link will be sent if the account exists. Customers should follow the instructions in the email and create a new password. If the email does not arrive, they should check spam or junk folders.

## 2. How can I change my registered email address?

Customers should open Account Settings → Profile → Email Address. For security, changing the email address requires verification of the new address and confirmation of account ownership. If the customer cannot access the existing account, support must verify the customer's identity before assisting.

## 3. Why is my account locked?

An account may be temporarily locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts or suspicious activity. Customers should wait for the temporary restriction to expire or use the password reset process. If the account remains locked, support should review the account and verify ownership before restoring access.

## 4. How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?

Customers can open Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication and follow the setup instructions. They should save any recovery codes securely. If they lose access to their authentication device, support must verify account ownership before helping restore access.

## 5. How can I update my profile information?

Customers can navigate to Account Settings → Profile and update supported profile fields such as display name and phone number. They should save the changes and verify that the updated information appears in their account.

## 6. I did not receive the account verification email. What should I do?

Customers should check their spam or junk folder and confirm that the registered email address is correct. They can request another verification email from the account verification screen. If verification still fails, support can investigate using the registered email address and account details.
