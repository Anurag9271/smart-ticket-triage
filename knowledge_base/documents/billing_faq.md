# NovaDesk Customer Support – Billing FAQ

> **Demo knowledge base:** NovaDesk is a fictional company used for this project. The policies below are sample policies created for testing the Smart Customer Support Ticket Triage & Auto-Responder.

## 1. Why was I charged twice for my subscription?

Duplicate charges may occur because of payment retries, pending bank authorizations, or multiple payment attempts. Customers should first verify whether both transactions are completed or whether one is still pending. If both charges are completed, the customer should contact support with the transaction dates and payment references. Support agents can verify the transactions and initiate a refund investigation.

## 2. How long does a refund take?

Under the NovaDesk demo policy, approved refunds are initiated within 3 business days. After initiation, the bank or payment provider may require an additional 5–10 business days to reflect the refund in the customer's account. The exact time may vary depending on the payment method and provider.

## 3. How can I download my subscription invoice?

Customers can download invoices from the Billing section of their account dashboard. They should select the relevant billing period and choose Download Invoice. If an invoice is missing, customers can contact support with the billing period and registered email address.

## 4. What subscription plans are available?

NovaDesk provides three demo subscription plans:

- **Basic:** Core application features for individual users.
- **Professional:** Advanced reporting and additional productivity features.
- **Enterprise:** Team-oriented features and administrative controls.

Customers can review their current subscription and plan details from the Billing section. The exact pricing is not specified in this demo knowledge base.

## 5. How can I cancel my subscription?

Customers can navigate to Account Settings → Billing → Manage Subscription and select Cancel Subscription. The cancellation confirmation should be reviewed before submitting. Under the demo policy, cancellation stops the next renewal; access remains available until the current paid period ends.

## 6. Why did my payment fail?

Payments may fail because of insufficient funds, expired cards, incorrect payment details, bank restrictions, or payment-provider issues. Customers should verify their payment details and contact their bank if necessary. If the issue continues, support can investigate the payment failure using the transaction reference and approximate payment time.
